Company Profile

Base Credit operates under the Base Credit legal entity and brand from Sweetwater, Tennessee. The current project and customer-facing site were established in 2026 to provide online account access, transaction visibility, support communications, admin-managed balance updates, and authenticated transfer workflows for users who prefer a banking-style interface with crypto-aware destination support.

The platform is presented for users in the United States and for activity that can be handled through the current web experience and support process. Access may be limited by account status, internal review, operational screening, geography, or risk controls, and the site should not be read as a promise of unrestricted availability in every use case.

What The Platform Does

Base Credit focuses on account administration and transaction coordination rather than broad public product marketing. Users can register, sign in, review balances and transaction history, manage profile settings, receive service emails, and submit authenticated transfer instructions from the account area.

  • Cash and payment services: account dashboards, balance views, transaction history, admin-reviewed balance adjustments, and authenticated transfer requests through wire-style and ACH-style workflows.
  • Crypto-related services: the current platform supports crypto-labeled transfer instructions and destination capture inside authenticated workflows. Any digital asset settlement should be understood as support-reviewed operational handling rather than open self-custody tooling exposed on the public site.
  • Lending or credit workflow: public loan and fee-collection flows are currently disabled. The live site does not present an open self-serve lending workflow.
  • Operational limitations: availability depends on account status, internal review, transfer eligibility, and support confirmation. Deprecated public simulator, loan, and payment flows remain unavailable unless formally redesigned and reviewed.

Operating Model And Controls

  • Customer funds: balances displayed in the dashboard are platform ledger values associated with customer accounts in the application. This page should not be read as a statement that Base Credit is a chartered bank or that customer balances carry insurance unless that disclosure appears in binding customer documents.
  • Digital asset handling: crypto-related transfer workflows currently collect and store destination details inside authenticated sessions. The public site does not expose self-custody wallet management, private-key control, or direct on-chain execution tooling to end users.
  • Compliance controls: account access is authenticated, transfer activity is limited to signed-in users, and operational review can be applied through admin controls, account status changes, balance adjustments, email notifications, and support escalation.
  • Risk disclosures: transfer activity may be delayed, limited, or declined based on review requirements. Crypto-related instructions may involve irreversible destinations, and users should confirm recipient details before submitting any request.

Mission And Governance

Base Credit is designed to present a clear, support-led customer account experience: users can review account information, track activity, and submit authenticated instructions from a single interface while operations staff manage review, status updates, and communications through the admin console.

Governance for the current platform centers on controlled access, documented user communications, and consistency across the dashboard, legal pages, and support responses. When product scope changes, the About page, Terms, Privacy Policy, and email templates should be updated together so customer-facing statements remain aligned.

Important Disclosures

  • Base Credit uses the regulatory status published in this project's current materials: Registered and Licensed.
  • The live web experience currently includes registration, sign-in, dashboard access, transaction history, support email notifications, and authenticated transfer workflows. Public loan, payment, and simulator pages are not active product offerings.
  • Partner banking, custody, payments, compliance, or liquidity counterparties are not named on this page unless they are separately disclosed in customer agreements or direct support materials.
  • Customer eligibility may be limited by geography, account review status, sanctions screening, fraud review, or operational requirements.
  • Interest, yield, rewards, and self-serve lending features are not guaranteed by this site and should not be inferred unless they are separately disclosed in current binding terms.
